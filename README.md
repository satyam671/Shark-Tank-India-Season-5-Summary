## 📂 The 10-Point Analysis Framework

Every markdown file in this repository corresponds to a specific episode and breaks down each pitch into these **10 Critical Dimensions**:

### 1. 🏢 Business Snapshot
* **The "Elevator Pitch":** What the business is about, the product/service offered, and the target audience.
* **The Founders:** Backgrounds, education, prior experience, and the unique story driving the brand.

### 2. 🚀 Goal & Vision
* The founders’ mission statement and where they see the company in the next 5-10 years.

### 3. 📊 Numbers That Matter (The Financials)
* *The heartbeat of the pitch.* We extract the hard data:
    * Revenue (Monthly/Yearly) & Run Rate
    * Profit Margins (Gross/Net)
    * CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) & LTV (Lifetime Value)
    * Burn Rate & Contribution Margin

### 4. 💰 Original Ask
* The valuation anchor. The exact investment amount and equity percentage asked by the pitchers.

### 5. 🤝 Final Deal
* **The Verdict:** What was actually offered, by which investor(s), and the final terms.
* **Structure:** Counter-offers, equity adjustments, royalty deals, or debt-equity mixes.

### 6. 🧠 Sharks’ Logic
* **The Why:** Why did investors accept or decline? (Valuation concerns, scalability, branding, clutter, etc.)
* **The Quotes:** Unique phrases, analogies, or mental models used by the sharks during the critique.

### 7. 📚 Key Business Terms in Action
* We list and explain the specific financial/business terms discussed (e.g., *Inventory Turnover*, *Pre-money Valuation*).
* **Application:** How these terms were applied specifically to the negotiation at hand.

### 8. ⚔️ Negotiation Highlights
* The dynamic back-and-forth.
* Counter-offers, leverage plays, and the psychology used to close (or kill) the deal.

### 9. 🌏 Market Context
* Industry size, competitors mentioned, and market trends discussed during the pitch.

### 10. 💡 Takeaway for Learners
* The "So What?" Practical lessons on numbers, clarity, and strategy for entrepreneurs and readers.

## 🤝 Contribution Guidelines

We want this to be the most comprehensive database of Indian startup pitches. If you spot a missing metric or a negotiation nuance we glossed over, please contribute!

### How to Contribute:

1.  **Fork** the repository.
2.  **Clone** your forked repo locally.
3.  **Create a Branch** (`git checkout -b feature/Episode-X-Update`).
4.  **Edit the Markdown** following the 10-Point Framework above.
5.  **Commit & Push** your changes.
6.  **Open a Pull Request** describing what you added.

### Contribution Rules:

* **Fact-Check:** Ensure numbers (Revenue, Ask, Valuation) are accurate to the episode airing.
* **Neutral Tone:** Keep the analysis objective. Report what happened, not what you *wish* happened.
* **Formatting:** Please adhere to the existing markdown structure for consistency.
